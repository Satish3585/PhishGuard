About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is an AI-powered phishing link and email detector designed to help users identify potentially malicious content before they click.

This tool checks for suspicious keywords, missing secure protocols, hidden IP addresses, urgent language, and generic greetings to determine the safety of an input.
